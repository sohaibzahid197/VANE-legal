Delete everything from inside the app

This is the only way, and it takes a few seconds:

  • Open VANE
  • Tap Settings
  • Tap Delete my data
  • Confirm Delete everything

What gets deleted

Everything we hold that is connected to your device:

  • Every call you have placed, graded or still open
  • Your accuracy, wins, losses and streak
  • Your crowd votes on every coin
  • Your notification token
  • Your anonymous account identifier

The deletion is immediate and permanent. Nothing is kept in a recoverable form. This cannot be undone.

What this does not do

It does not affect the market signals, which are public, identical for everyone, and contain nothing about you.

Aggregate crowd percentages already published — for example "64% up" — are counts, not records of individuals, and are not recalculated retroactively. Your vote stops being counted from the next refresh.

It does not uninstall the app. If you open VANE again it will create a brand new anonymous identity with an empty record.

Why we cannot delete it for you by email

VANE has no sign-up, no email address and no password by design. Your account is an anonymous identifier held on your device, and we have no way to connect an email to it. That means we genuinely cannot verify that a record is yours, and deleting on request would mean deleting a stranger's data on a stranger's say-so.

The in-app button is the erasure route, and it is always available. If it fails, it will tell you why rather than silently doing nothing.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Reinstalling creates a new identity, so the button will not reach your old records. Those records are a random identifier with a handful of market calls attached and nothing that identifies you — no name, no email, no device id, no location. If you would like them removed anyway, email us and we will explain what we can and cannot do in your case.

Using VANE without any record

You can read every signal without ever placing a call or casting a vote. If you never do either, the only thing stored is an empty anonymous identifier.
