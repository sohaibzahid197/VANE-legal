The short version

VANE does not ask for your name, your email address or a password. It signs you in anonymously so your own calls can follow you, and that is the only thing tied to you. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and there is no analytics or advertising software in the app.

What we store

  • An anonymous account identifier. Created automatically on first launch. It is a random string. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or device owner.
  • The calls you place — the coin, the direction, the horizon, the price at the time, and the result once it is graded.
  • Your crowd votes — one per coin, changeable at any time.
  • A notification token, only if you allow notifications, so a message can reach your device.
  • Your settings — your watchlist and your alert preferences.

The signals themselves are public and identical for everyone, so reading them stores nothing about you.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address or password. There is no sign-up form in the app.
  • Payment details. There is no purchase in the app.
  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers or crash-reporting profiles.

Where it is stored

Your calls, votes and settings are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, part of Google Firebase, under your anonymous identifier. Data is encrypted in transit. The most recent signals are also cached on your device so the app still shows something useful when you are offline.

Who else can see it

Nobody, other than Google as our hosting provider. Your individual calls and votes are readable only by your own device. The crowd percentages shown in the app are aggregate counts — how many people said up or down — and a coin only shows a percentage once enough people have voted that no single person can be identified from it.

We do not share, sell or rent your data. We disclose information only if the law requires it.

Notifications

The daily digest is scheduled on your device, so it needs no server and no data leaves your phone for it. If you allow notifications we store a delivery token so future messages can reach you. Turning notifications off in your device settings stops this.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete my data in the app. That permanently erases your calls, your track record and your votes from our servers, and cannot be undone. Because the account is anonymous, we cannot delete it on request by email — we would have no way to tell which records were yours. See the Delete Data page.

Children

VANE deals with financial markets and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
