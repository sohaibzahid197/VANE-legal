What it does

VANE shows a direction and a price target for 30 major coins, refreshed through the day. You can call the market yourself and the app grades you automatically, so you can see whether you actually beat the model.

  • Signals for 30 coins at 24h, 7d and 30d horizons
  • Real price history and an uncertainty band on every forecast
  • Crowd sentiment — what other people think, next to what the model thinks
  • Your own calls, graded automatically when the horizon closes
  • A daily digest notification, scheduled on your device

Signals are often wrong

VANE is not financial advice and it is not a licensed advisory service. The model is a transparent statistical score, not a crystal ball, and it does not reliably beat a simple baseline. Treat every number in the app as one opinion among many.

Never invest more than you can afford to lose.

Help

Do I need an account?

No. VANE signs you in anonymously the first time you open it, so your calls and your track record follow you without an email address or a password. There is nothing to remember and nothing to lose.

Why did my track record disappear?

Your anonymous identity lives on the device. Signing out, deleting the app or wiping its data starts a new one, and the old record cannot be recovered because there is no password to sign back in with.

How do I get the daily digest?

Open Settings → Notifications and allow notifications. The digest is scheduled on your device for 08:00 local time, so it works in any timezone and without a connection.

How often do prices update?

Roughly every 30 minutes. The Signals screen shows how long ago the data arrived, and tells you plainly when it is showing something it could not refresh.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. See the Delete Data page.
